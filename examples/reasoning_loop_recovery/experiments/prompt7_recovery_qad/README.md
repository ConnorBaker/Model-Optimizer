# Prompt-7 recovery QAD

Test whether a short recovery QAD run on completed responses generated by the **NVFP4 QAD
Qwen3.6-35B-A3B checkpoint after 500 QAD iterations**, using prompt 7, reduces termination failures
when the anti-repetition instruction is removed at evaluation time.

This experiment uses **334 training examples and 42 QAD iterations** on Qwen3.6-35B-A3B.

**Original QAD** means the NVFP4 QAD Qwen3.6-35B-A3B checkpoint after 500 QAD iterations,
before recovery training. **Recovery QAD** means the checkpoint after the additional 42 iterations
on the prompt-7-generated dataset.

The YAML files **record the settings used in the completed experiment**. They are not yet consumed
by scripts in this directory and cannot be passed directly to SciCode, Megatron Bridge, or ModelOpt
Launcher.

Generated datasets and model checkpoints are supplied separately; they are not included in this directory.

## Create the training dataset

Generate responses with prompt 7, filter them for completion and repetition, then remove prompt 7
from the training inputs. This retained 334 of 440 training candidates.

See [data/README.md](data/README.md) for generation settings, the exact prompt, filtering rules,
dataset formatting, and provenance.

## Recovery QAD training and NVFP4 export

Continue QAD on the 334 prepared examples, then export the recovery checkpoint to NVFP4.
See [training/README.md](training/README.md) for the objective, training and export settings,
command-line options, and runtime compatibility notes.

## Evaluate without prompt 7

Compare original QAD and recovery QAD on the same 55 subtasks and ten seeds per subtask, using
the original SciCode prompt without prompt 7 or a presence penalty.

See [evaluation/README.md](evaluation/README.md) for the custom SciCode runner, comparison settings,
and metric definitions.

## Results

The [result summary](results/summary.yaml) stores the metrics below and additional token statistics.

Recomputed from saved per-response metrics, checking the complete unique `(subtask, seed)` set.
`Both` is the intersection of cap hits and missing final answers.

### Training subtasks: 44 × 10 attempts

| Model | Cap hits | No final | Both | Correct |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original QAD | 41/440 (9.32%) | 42/440 | 40/440 | 57/440 |
| Prompt-7-data QAD | 17/440 (3.86%) | 15/440 | 15/440 | 57/440 |

Cap-hit rate decreased from **9.32% to 3.86%**. Mean completion tokens decreased from 23,677 to 18,767.

### Held-out subtasks: 11 × 10 attempts

| Model | Cap hits | No final | Both | Correct |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Original QAD | 12/110 (10.91%) | 12/110 | 12/110 | 6/110 |
| Prompt-7-data QAD | 7/110 (6.36%) | 7/110 | 7/110 | 7/110 |

Cap-hit rate decreased from **10.91% to 6.36%**. Mean completion tokens decreased from 23,742 to 19,231.

These are selected failure-prone subtasks and a small held-out sample, with possible parent-problem
overlap. They do not establish a benchmark-wide rate, guaranteed recovery, or an accuracy improvement.
Prompt 7's individual contribution is not isolated by this comparison: there is no original-QAD,
original-prompt recovery-data control in this experiment.

## Reproduction requirements and compatibility

These files record a completed experiment; **a turnkey rerun on current `main` is not yet available**. The custom
SciCode request runner, original generated data, model checkpoints, full experiment-local distillation
entrypoint, and runtime patches are not packaged here. Public artifact download URLs have not yet
been supplied. The frozen split and prompt are included; source fingerprints identify omitted code.

See [training runtime compatibility](training/README.md#runtime-compatibility) for the validated
environment and differences from current `main`.
